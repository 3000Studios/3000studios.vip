Thunder Dome Offline Beta Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026. This policy covers the offline gameplay beta of Thunderdome: AeroStrike (Thunder Dome), Android package com.studios3000.thunderdome, provided by 3000 Studios.

Gameplay data stays on your device

The offline beta stores your pilot callsign, campaign progress, aircraft upgrades, earned game currency, scores, and settings locally on your Android device. The game does not automatically transmit this information to 3000 Studios or another service.

This beta has no account creation, Google sign-in, cloud synchronization, online multiplayer, advertising, in-app purchases, or remote analytics. The app does not request Internet access. Local game currency has no cash value.

Optional sharing

If you choose Share after a sortie, Android's share chooser sends your pilot callsign, stage, aircraft, score, and kills to the app you select. That app handles the information under its own privacy policy. Sharing is optional; 3000 Studios does not automatically receive these results.

Removing your local data

You can delete the beta's local saves and settings through Android Settings → Apps → Thunder Dome → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage permanently removes local progress. This offline beta creates no online account to delete.

Optional support and feedback

If you choose to email support@3000studios.vip, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate game issues. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Support messages are retained while needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Email the same address to request access, correction, or deletion of support information we hold. We do not sell your personal information.

External services

Google Play processes installation and store-related information under Google's policies. Opening this privacy page or sending email uses your browser or email app, which may process technical information under its own policy. Website processing is explained in the 3000 Studios website privacy policy.

Age and future updates

This beta is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. Online services or purchases will require an updated privacy policy and store declarations before they are introduced.
