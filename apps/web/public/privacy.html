This policy covers https://3000studios.vip and related 3000 Studios pages, including the official music promo tools on this site (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting, YouTube embeds, Instagram / Reels links, DistroKid HyperFollow, Spotify links, and Google AdSense). Who we are

3000 Studios is an independent music and media label operated by Jeremy Swain. Contact: mr.jwswain@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact messages. The contact form sends your message (name, email address, topic, and message text) to our studio inbox so we can reply. If the inbox cannot be reached, the form opens your own email app with the message addressed to us instead.
  • On-device preferences. Playback settings, your cookie-consent choice, and unlock entitlements are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Technical and security data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and approximate region to deliver the site, keep it secure, and prevent abuse.
  • If you tap Connect TikTok on /tiktok: TikTok Login Kit may share a user identifier, the basic public profile fields TikTok allows, and permission to upload a promo video as a draft or (after TikTok app review) a public post. We do not sell that token. It is used only to post 3000 Studios music clips you already own.
  • YouTube: videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode, which limits tracking until you play a video. Studio uploads use Google OAuth on our own machines, not visitor Google accounts, unless you explicitly sign in to a 3000 Studios tool.
  • Instagram, Facebook, X, and similar: outbound links only unless you later authorize an official API. We do not scrape your messages.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and secure the site; stream music and video; measure traffic; show ads (with your consent, as described below); answer contact, booking, and press messages; process merch orders; and promote official releases on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and our own pages. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and advertising

Necessary cookies and local storage keep the site working (preferences, consent choice, playback). With your consent, Google AdSense and similar partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve and measure ads. You can change your consent choice at any time in your browser. Opt out of personalized advertising: Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

Payments

Merch and other purchases check out through Stripe or PayPal payment links. Payment card details are entered on and processed by the payment provider — we never see or store your card number.

Platforms and embedded media

When you use TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Spotify, Apple Music, or DistroKid from our links, those companies process data under their own policies. TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting follow TikTok's developer terms. We only request user.info.basic and video.upload until Direct Post is approved; then we may also use video.publish for official 3000 Studios clips.

Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the site: Cloudflare (hosting and security), Google (advertising), and Stripe / PayPal (payments). We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Contact email is kept as long as needed to reply and for legal records. On-device preferences remain in your browser until you clear them. OAuth tokens for promo live on our studio systems, not in public pages. Server logs rotate on Cloudflare's defaults.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Email us to exercise these rights. You can disconnect TikTok in TikTok's app settings. California / GDPR-style requests go to the same email.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
